04

How to read every number

The tab marks each figure with its basis. A cost we have not measured is never shown as if we had.

Measured

Summed from bills and records. Safe to quote.

Estimated

A rate × a quantity. The rate is an assumption set by hand.

Unpriced

No rate set, so it reads $0. Margin is an upper bound.

Needs fixing

Live and displayed, but known to be incorrect.

Live, but resting on assumptions

Real arithmetic on a rate somebody set by hand. Each needs sign-off.

  • Product COGSNeeds batch actuals Only 72 of 214 orders use real build cost. The other 142 use the roadmap schedule — 79% of COGS dollars.
  • Support labour rate$75/hr, unconfirmed A blended person-hour never reconciled to payroll.
  • Return handling rates$50 + 1hr, unconfirmed Stocking fee and inspection time are both assumptions. Return freight is missing for most returns.
  • 3PL handlingNo invoice reconciled Contracted rate card applied to 22 orders. Never checked against a FlexSpace bill.
  • Exchange rateOne fixed rate A single hand-set rate applied to all history, so older USD orders are restated at today's rate.

09

Known to be wrong

Two issues. Both are being displayed today and both need fixing before the number is used.

  • Instalment orders are each charged a full machine's build cost≈ $19,511 overstated Monthly payment plans arrive as separate orders, and nothing distinguishes an instalment from a full purchase. Affects 16 customers across 44 orders. Correcting it moves the headline margin from −$15,680 to roughly +$3,831. It also inflates the freight gap and distorts revenue per order.
  • Acquisition cost is $0 for every customer$69,133.81 stranded The Meta sync pulls lifetime campaign totals instead of a monthly breakdown, so all spend collapses into a single month in which no customer was acquired. CAC, LTV:CAC and payback are blank across the whole tab.

Summary

Safe to present

  • Revenue, discounts and tax
  • Refunds
  • Consumables and parts
  • Invoiced freight
  • Warranty claim rate
  • US vs Canada split
  • The model's structure

Do not present as fact

  • The −$15,680.31 headline margin
  • Any CAC, LTV:CAC or payback figure
  • COGS at the order level
  • Revenue per order
  • Province-level rankings
  • Margins for instalment customers

The dashboard is built, wired and tested. It currently reports a loss on every machine, which we have good reason to believe is not true. Two fixes and three numbers from Finance turn it into something we can run the business on.
